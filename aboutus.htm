Sonny Scaffolds, Inc. has been building quality scaffolding, drywall carts and painters scaffolds since 1985. The Company was founded by Sonny Perry and Dick Blake who had a long history of scaffold manufacturing and design back to the era of the original Baker Scaffold. The company is known for its innovations, sound technical design, safety characteristics and practical applications. Sonny provides products at pricing that is very competitive in the market place.

The Indianapolis based company distributes its products throughout the U.S. via a network of over 600 dealers. the Company also distributes products worldwide.

Sonny Scaffolds was acquired in the late 2004 resulting in the injection of substantial energy and resources into the company, optimizing its potential. Sonny products are now advertised in many trade publications. New dealers are being added to the Company roster weekly. The Company has worked to establish itself with a strong presence in all geographic locations of the United States. Additionally, the company added new product lines including the Guardian Medium Duty Scaffold and the new innovative "Stow-N-Go" Dry Wall Cart.

Built on principles of superior quality and long-lasting durability, the complete Sonny family of products continues to position our Company as the
